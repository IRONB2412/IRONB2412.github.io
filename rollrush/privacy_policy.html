Roll Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Roll Rush (the "App"), published by IronBCorporation ("we"), handles information. We wrote it to be short and clear.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. We have no servers that receive your data.

Your game progress (level, coins, unlocked balls, settings) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, directly on Google's side:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your device settings),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, ads viewed or tapped, crash and performance data of the ads SDK).

This information is used for advertising, ad measurement, analytics of ad performance and fraud prevention. How Google uses data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland and regulated US states: the App shows Google's consent message the first time you play. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options inside the App.
  • Personalized ads: you can opt out of ad personalization or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

4. In-app purchase

You can buy the optional Remove Ads upgrade. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The App only receives confirmation of the purchase, so ads can stay off and the purchase can be restored if you reinstall.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, the App shows reminders (for example, when your daily reward is ready). They are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to us or to a notification server. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

6. Internet connection

The App requires an internet connection so ads can load. To check the connection, the App sends a simple request to Google's connectivity endpoint (generate_204); no personal data is sent with it.

7. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, you can delete the App from the device; we hold no data to remove.

8. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device and is stored in an encrypted file. Data processed by Google is handled under Google's policies and is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
