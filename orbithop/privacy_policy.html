Orbit Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how the web game Orbit Hop (the "Game"), published by IronBCorporation ("we"), handles information. The Game is made for YouTube Playables.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. We run no servers that receive your data, and the Game contains no analytics or tracking code.

2. Saved progress

Your progress (best score, stardust, unlocked skins, sound and vibration settings) is saved:

  • when played on YouTube: through YouTube's own Playables save feature, handled by YouTube;
  • when played on this website: only in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

3. Advertising

When played on YouTube, the Game may show ads provided by YouTube through the YouTube Playables SDK. We do not use any other ad network. Ads and any data used for them are handled by YouTube and Google under the Google Privacy Policy. On this website no real ads are shown.

4. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
